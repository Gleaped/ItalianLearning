# CELI 3 (B2): exam format summary

The CELI 3 is the B2 certificate of the Università per Stranieri di Perugia (CVCL). This summary was checked on 2026-11-06 against the official PDFs listed at the end. Details marked **(non verificato)** were not in any official source we could read. They are our own advice or common practice.

## Structure and timing

| Booklet | Part | Task | Format | Items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fascicolo 1, **2 h 15 min** (A + B together) | A, Comprensione della lettura | A.1 | two texts, multiple choice with four options | 9 (5 + 4) |
| | | A.2 | "due testi a confronto": statements matched to text 1 or text 2 (binary choice) | 10 |
| | | A.3 | one text with open questions | 4 |
| | B, Produzione di testi scritti | B.1 | short composition on personal experience or a topic of general interest, chosen from 2 prompts | 1 (120–180 words) |
| | | B.2 | strongly contextualised letter or email, chosen from 3 prompts | 1 (80–100 words) |
| Fascicolo 2, **45 min** | C, Competenza linguistica | C.1 | two texts to complete (cloze) | 23 |
| | | C.2 | "frasi da ricostruire": rebuild sentences by linking them correctly | 7 |
| | | C.3 | complete sentences with a word derived from a given word of the same family | 5 |
| Fascicolo 3, **25 min** | D, Comprensione dell'ascolto | D.1 | two recordings, multiple choice with four options | 10 (5 + 5) |
| | | D.2 | complete sentences with words from the recording | 10 |
| Orale, **about 15 min** | Produzione orale | – | short self-presentation, then: describe and discuss a photo, summarise a short text and discuss it, role-play (compito comunicativo) | 3 materials, received **about 15 min before** for preparation |

How many times each recording is played, the length of the texts and any word limit on A.3 answers are **(non verificato)**. The official specification does not state them. One prep book (Edilingua) says A.3 has a word limit but gives no number.

## Scoring

| Part | Raw marks | Converted points | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A.1: 3 per correct answer; A.2: 1 per correct answer; A.3: 3 for correct, 2 for partly correct, 1 for incomplete (raw maximum 49) | 40 | – |
| B | B.1: 0–20, B.2: 0–20 (analytic scales) | 40 | – |
| C | C.1: 1 per item; C.2: 2 for a correct sentence, 1 for incomplete or badly built; C.3: 1 per item (raw maximum 42) | 20 | 10% |
| D | D.1: 3 per answer; D.2: 1 per completion | 40 | 20% |
| Orale | 0–20 × 3 | 60 | 30% |

- Written total (A + B + C + D) = **140**; oral = **60**; overall = **200**.
- **Minimum to pass: 84/140 in the written part and 33/60 in the oral, and 117/200 overall.**
- Grades: A *ottimo* 173–200; B *buono* 144–172; C *sufficiente* 117–143 (passing); D 69–116 and E 0–68 (failing).
- **Capitalizzazione:** if you pass only the written or only the oral part, the passed part stays valid for one year (two sessions). You then retake only the other part.

## Writing criteria (B.1 and B.2, 20 points each)

| Criterion | B.1 | B.2 | Band 5 (top) | Middle band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competenza lessicale | 5 | 5 | vocabulary good and always appropriate, rare spelling errors | almost always appropriate, sometimes repetitive, up to 4 spelling errors |
| Competenza sociolinguistica (moduli espressivi, register) | 5 | **6** | expressions always correct and appropriate | errors never make the expressions inappropriate |
| Competenza grammaticale (strutture) | 5 | **4** | varied, well-linked structures, rare errors | simple structures, up to 4 errors, sentences not always well linked |
| Coerenza e coesione | 5 | 5 | follows every instruction in the prompt, good logical order and cohesion | small omissions that don't affect the task, cohesion just sufficient |

Leaving out a point required by the prompt costs marks under *coerenza e coesione* ("one or more instructions missing" = 2/5).

## Speaking criteria (1–5 each, maximum 20, multiplied by 3)

- **Competenza lessicale:** top band "good and always appropriate, rare errors"; band 3 "almost always appropriate, sometimes repetitive".
- **Competenza grammaticale:** top band "a certain variety of structures, rare errors"; band 3 "simple structures, several errors".
- **Competenza sociolinguistica:** top band language always appropriate, active, fluent and spontaneous participation; band 3 sometimes inappropriate, some hesitation.
- **Competenza fonetica:** top band good, clear pronunciation and natural intonation; band 3 several errors, uncertain rhythm.

Examiner guide (vademecum): two examiners take part. E1 talks with you and E2 scores. Examiners use *Lei*. The self-presentation comes first, and you may usually choose the order of the other parts. An error repeated several times counts once. Errors in structures above the level are not penalised. Opinions and personality do not affect the score.

## What the exam expects (official competence description)

- **Reading:** authentic texts, mostly journalism (articles, interviews, news, letters to newspapers, adverts, surveys) and non-fiction or fiction about contemporary society. You need the gist, specific details and simple inferences.
- **Writing:** a simple but coherent and cohesive composition (experiences, descriptions, social topics), plus everyday formal and informal letters (asking for or giving information, advice or opinions, complaints, plans) and short messages or ads.
- **Grammar for Part C:** indicative tenses; present and past conditional (politeness, wishes, advice, hypotheses); imperative including the formal form; passive and impersonal forms; common uses of the congiuntivo (in complement clauses and indirect questions, in the periodo ipotetico); infinitive with prepositions; gerund (cause, manner, time); main coordinating and subordinating connectives.
- **Listening:** radio and TV (announcements, adverts, news, programmes), phone-ins, interviews, talk shows and short monologues, close to standard Italian with mild regional accents. You need the situation, how it develops, and the speakers' feelings and intentions.
