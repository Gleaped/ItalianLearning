# Course outline: from B1 to C1

This outline is for Richard to review. The app reads the same structure from `app/src/main/assets/course/course.json`.

## How the course works

- **Lesson length:** each lesson takes about 10 minutes and is fully structured: warm-up and retrieval → short grammar note → controlled practice → listening or reading → speaking → (in the last lesson) a writing task.
- **Unit size:** each unit has 3 lessons (about 30 minutes) and 15–25 vocabulary items, which go into the spaced-repetition review deck automatically.
- **Every lesson includes speaking:** repeat-after-me, an open spoken answer or a role-play.
- **Grammar and communication together:** every unit has one grammar focus and one real-life goal (appointments, offices, neighbours, shops, school, work, social life).
- **Checkpoints (Ripasso):** after every 3 content units there is a review unit. It recycles earlier grammar and vocabulary in mixed tasks and ends with a longer speaking and writing task. Use it to check whether you're ready to move on.
- **Remedial A2 (Ripasso A2):** short refreshers you can open at any time. Each B1 unit links to the A2 units it depends on (`remedialUnitIds`). If a B1 warm-up feels shaky, do the linked refresher first.
- **Exercise types used:** grammar notes, flashcards, multiple choice, gap fill, conjugation tables, sentence building, translation in both directions, matching, error correction, dictation, listening comprehension, reading comprehension, repeat-aloud, spoken answers, role-plays and free writing with a self-check list.

C1 has 5 content units between its two checkpoints, not 3. Its later units are more about range and register than new grammar, so they recycle each other heavily.

## Unit 1 in detail: `b1-01` Com'è andata?

**Objective:** tell someone how a recent day, appointment or weekend went, with the imperfetto for the scene and the passato prossimo for what happened.
**Remedial link:** `a2r-01` Il passato prossimo.

| Lesson | Focus | Speaking |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Com'era? L'imperfetto | Imperfetto forms (essere and fare tables) and uses: time, weather, states, habits, descriptions | Repeat aloud; describe your childhood home |
| 2. Mentre aspettavo… | mentre + imperfetto vs quando + passato prossimo; bounded duration (*ho aspettato due ore*) | Repeat aloud; tell about a small mishap |
| 3. Racconta com'è andata | Verbs that change meaning: *ho conosciuto* (met), *ho saputo* (found out), *dovevo* vs *sono dovuto*; reading a message about the anagrafe | Role-play with a neighbour about the weekend; then write a 60–90-word message |

Vocabulary (23 items): narrative connectors (*mentre, all'improvviso, a un certo punto, alla fine, per fortuna, nel frattempo*) and office and appointment language (*la sala d'attesa, il numerino, lo sportello, l'anagrafe, la fototessera, scadere*).

## Assessment note

Checkpoints are self-assessment tools. They are not formal CEFR certification. For an official certificate, the CILS (Università per Stranieri di Siena) and CELI (Università per Stranieri di Perugia) exams exist at B1, B2 and C1. Check their current formats on the official sites before preparing for one.
