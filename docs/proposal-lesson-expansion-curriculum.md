# Curriculum map: 270 units, A1 to C2

Companion to [proposal-lesson-expansion.md](proposal-lesson-expansion.md). Each unit has 5 lessons in format 2 (warm-up, learn, practise, use) and 15 to 30 vocabulary items. Format of each entry: `title (grammar or skill focus)`. Existing units are marked with the id they come from: **(a1-03)** means the unit is kept, extended or rewritten from that existing unit. Level checks and checkpoint units are counted in the totals.

Counts: A1 30, A2 36, B1 48, B2 60, C1 60, C2 36 = **270**.

## A1 (30 units, 150 lessons, about 700 new words)

## How the existing 71 units map

- **Kept almost as is (about 30):** the B1 to C1 units that are already format 2 and need only ids, tags and tier data.
- **Split or extended (about 25):** A1 and A2 units that become two or three units each to reach the full beginner path; B1 and B2 units that gain a second lesson set.
- **Folded into tracks (about 16):** celi-01 to celi-10 into B2-9, cg-01 to cg-08 into B1-3, and the speaking units into the speaking library.
- Content with real teaching value is never deleted. Anything replaced is moved to `course/archive/` for one release.
